## Detailed Checks

### 1. Palantir cost structure is negotiated and enterprise-shaped
Status:
- Confirmed

What supports it:
- Palantir `2025 FY 10-K` says Palantir has changed pricing models in the past and may change them again.
- The same filing says larger organizations may demand `different pricing structures` and `substantial price concessions`.
- It also states Palantir platforms are generally offered on a `productized basis`, contrasted with labor-based procurement.

What this means:
- Pricing should be described as customer-specific and contract-shaped.
- A single seat-based number is not a credible way to describe current enterprise Foundry/AIP adoption.

Sources:
- Palantir 2025 FY 10-K  
  https://investors.palantir.com/files/2025%20FY%20PLTR%2010-K.pdf

### 2. Palantir has publicly visible component pricing, but not a full modern enterprise price sheet
Status:
- Confirmed

What supports it:
- GSA awarded federal pricelist effective 2024-02-06 lists:
  - monthly cloud license per core: `USD 7,050.77`
  - monthly term license per core: `USD 7,050.77`
  - perpetual license per core: `USD 141,015.42`
  - appliance per core: `USD 151,042.82`
  - annual support per core: `USD 28,203.08` or `USD 37,753.85`
  - Bootcamp / developer / integrator training prices
  - engineering and implementation hourly rates

Important caveat:
- This is not a complete current commercial Foundry/AIP price card.
- It is still useful as a fact anchor for how Palantir decomposes software, support, training, and implementation.

Sources:
- GSA awarded federal pricelist  
  https://www.gsaadvantage.gov/ref_text/47QTCA24D004L/103TVU.3VU6QI_47QTCA24D004L_PALANTIR47QTCA24D004L.PDF

### 3. Palantir frequently converts bootcamps / pilots into large ACV deals
Status:
- Confirmed

What supports it:
- Palantir `Q3 2024 Business Update` includes commercial journey examples such as:
  - `50 Days to 7-Figure ACV Deal`
  - `<2 Months to 7-Figure ACV Deal`
  - `Initial Bootcamp`
  - `Enterprise-Wide expansions in discussion`

What this means:
- A realistic Palantir cost narrative should include bootcamp / scoping / pilot as an acquisition and expansion mechanism.

Sources:
- Palantir Q3 2024 Business Update  
  https://investors.palantir.com/files/Palantir%20Q3%202024%20Business%20Update.pdf

### 5. Samsung selected Palantir for the DX customer-facing rollout
Status:
- Refuted

What supports it:
- ETNews reported on `2024-09-02` that Samsung selected `Microsoft` for the customer-facing AI chatbot rollout for Samsung.com and retail stores.

Implication:
- It is inaccurate to say Samsung broadly adopted Palantir for that specific DX customer-facing initiative.

Sources:
- ETNews, 2024-09-02  
  https://www.etnews.com/20240902000267

### 6. Samsung DS / Foundry publicly confirmed a Palantir deployment tied to yield improvement
Status:
- Unconfirmed

What was found:
- A secondary article on `2025-03-18` from TechPowerUp says The Korea Economic Daily reported Samsung had partnered with Palantir to improve semiconductor yield, quality, and productivity.
- In this pass, I did not recover a directly accessible original KED/KED Global article or any Samsung / Palantir primary source confirming that claim.

Why this stays unconfirmed:
- The accessible source chain is weak.
- No official Samsung IR or Palantir release found here attributes semiconductor yield improvement to Palantir.

How to use this in reporting:
- At most: `secondary-source rumor / unverified press signal`.
- Do not present as an established fact.

Source reviewed:
- TechPowerUp, 2025-03-18  
  https://www.techpowerup.com/334276/samsung-reportedly-partnered-up-with-palantir-to-improve-chip-production-yields

### 7. Samsung Foundry improved during 2025
Status:
- Confirmed

What supports it:
- Samsung official 1Q 2025 material says Foundry earnings were weak, fab utilization was stagnant, and the company aimed to stabilize 2nm and secure major orders.
- Samsung official 2Q 2025 material says Foundry revenue grew significantly but earnings remained weak due to export restrictions and prolonged low utilization at mature nodes; it also says Samsung planned to ramp GAA 2nm mass production and improve utilization.
- Samsung official 3Q 2025 material says Foundry achieved `record-high customer orders` on advanced nodes, started mass production of first-gen 2nm GAA, and improved earnings as utilization improved and one-off costs subsided.

Implication:
- Samsung Foundry recovery / improvement through 2025 is real.
- But the cause cannot be assigned to Palantir on the basis of these materials.

  - it signed a strategic partnership with Palantir

## Reporting Guidance

### Safe statements
- Palantir pricing is negotiated, enterprise-shaped, and commonly bundled with services, training, and implementation.
- Samsung DX publicly tested Palantir in 2024, but Microsoft was publicly reported as the selected vendor for that customer-facing rollout.
- Samsung Foundry performance improved through 2025, but public materials do not support attributing that improvement to Palantir.
- LG now has a current, public, source-backed Palantir path through LG CNS.
- Samsung DS AI Center shows a strong ontology / knowledge graph hiring signal, including explicit reference to Palantir ontology.

### Unsafe statements
- Samsung has publicly confirmed Palantir in Foundry production.
- Samsung 2nm yield improvement was caused by Palantir.
- SK has a confirmed Palantir manufacturing deployment.
- Palantir pricing can be reduced to a simple per-seat figure.
