# Deep Research Prompt

다음 자료를 바탕으로 `Display Week와 블루 OLED 관련 업데이트`에 대한 심층 기술 리뷰를 작성하라.

## Topic
- Display Week를 앞둔 시점에서 blue OLED / blue PHOLED / PSF OLED 관련 산업 신호와 연구 신호를 함께 해석

## Audience
- 디스플레이 소재/소자 연구자
- OLED 제품/패널 전략 담당자
- 기술전략 리더
- 투자/사업 검토에 참여하는 기술기획 인력

## Required Outcome
- `무엇이 실제로 검증되었는가`
- `무엇은 아직 연구 단계인가`
- `Display Week 현장에서 어떤 질문을 던져야 하는가`
- `LG Display / UDC / recent literature`를 하나의 evidence ladder로 정리

## Source Priority
1. 업로드된 source note, prior local package, Pulse capture
2. LG Display official announcement distributed via PR Newswire
3. UDC official IR press release
4. Display Week official program page
5. RSC / Advanced Materials / Nature Photonics peer-reviewed papers
6. 필요한 경우에만 보조 기사나 2차 요약

## Facts To Anchor Explicitly
- `2025-04-30` LG Display announced commercialization-level verification of blue phosphorescent OLED panels on a mass production line.
- The disclosed structure was a `hybrid two-stack Tandem OLED` with blue fluorescence in one stack and blue phosphorescence in the other.
- LG Display said power consumption was about `15% lower` while maintaining a similar stability level to existing OLED panels.
- LG Display said it would showcase the panel at `SID Display Week 2025`.
- `2026-02-26` UDC and LG Display announced an extension of long-term material supply and license agreements.
- A 2026 RSC paper reported `stable pure-blue phosphor-sensitized OLEDs utilizing a hypsochromic-shifting non-TADF fluorescent emitter`.
- A 2025 Advanced Materials paper reported that the `polariton-enhanced Purcell effect` increased deep-blue PSF OLED lifetime by about `3.1x` at `10 mA cm^-2`.
- A 2025 Nature Photonics paper reported tandem PEP-PHOLED results with much stronger lifetime metrics than earlier single-junction comparators.

## Research Questions
1. Does the LG Display result amount to commercialization of `pure-blue PHOLED`, or only commercialization-level verification of a hybrid architecture?
2. How should the UDC-LGD agreement extension be interpreted: routine renewal, or a meaningful confidence signal around the roadmap?
3. What technical gap is each paper trying to close:
   - pure-blue color purity
   - triplet utilization
   - lifetime
   - roll-off
   - cavity and exciton-density management
4. What is the most accurate technical interpretation of the current state:
   - `blue PHOLED solved`
   - `hybrid commercialization path is emerging`
   - `PSF / PEP are reducing the remaining barrier`
   - or another synthesis
5. If an expert attends Display Week now, what diligence checklist best separates real progress from over-reading?

## Comparison Axes
- industry announcement vs peer-reviewed paper
- hybrid two-stack phosphorescent architecture vs pure-blue PSF route
- panel/process readiness vs molecule/device physics readiness
- power savings vs lifetime vs color purity
- commercialization evidence vs roadmap evidence

## Writing Rules
- Use exact dates.
- Separate `fact`, `interpretation`, and `uncertainty`.
- Do not flatten the distinction between `hybrid commercialization verification` and `direct pure-blue PHOLED mass commercialization`.
- Be explicit about which claims are supported only by company statements and which are supported by peer-reviewed metrics.
- End with:
  - a concise thesis
  - a Display Week question list
  - a next-step recommendation for a technical strategy team
