# Deep Research Prompt

## Topic
Analyze the April 10, 2026 `AI Updates Weekly` video and companion slide deck as a discovery layer, then produce a technically rigorous review of what actually matters across agent platforms, model ecosystem shifts, developer tooling, and labor signals.

## Audience
- Engineers
- technical product managers
- research leads
- strategy and planning stakeholders who need an evidence-based AI market update

## Research Objective
Do not summarize the video naively. Use it as an index of claims to validate. Distinguish:
- verified platform changes
- credible but still early ecosystem signals
- speculative or weakly supported claims

## Seed Materials
- YouTube video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tILZuOvro6I
- Companion deck extracted from the creator repository and slide package
- local transcript and slide extract in this topic folder

## What Must Be Verified
1. Anthropic Managed Agents launch timing, pricing model, and enterprise significance
2. Claude Microsoft 365 connector availability and scope
3. Google Gemma 4 positioning, sizes, licensing, and practical relevance
4. Meta Muse Spark product positioning and whether it is open or closed
5. Microsoft MAI model announcements and why they matter
6. Cursor 3.0 product change and workflow impact
7. OpenClaw and Claude Code release cadence and what those releases suggest about the desktop-agent category
8. MemPalace and the local-memory pattern around markdown, vector DB, and personal knowledge systems
9. Anthropic Mythos Preview and what it really is
10. AI labor-market signals from layoffs and software-job openings data

## Required Source Priority
1. Official vendor blogs, docs, support pages, repos, or release notes
2. Maintainer repositories and release pages
3. Credible reporting for legal or labor topics
4. The weekly video and companion deck only as the discovery and framing layer

## Core Questions
1. What is the main structural theme behind this week of updates?
2. Which items change near-term engineering workflow in a concrete way?
3. Which items matter for enterprise adoption and governance?
4. Which claims are overstated in the original video framing?
5. What should a technically sophisticated reader watch over the next 30 to 90 days?

## Deliverables
- concise executive summary
- categorized analysis by theme
- table or structured comparison across major items
- explicit fact versus interpretation separation
- practical implications for builders, teams, and technical decision makers
- clear uncertainty notes where evidence is weak

## Output Style
- exact dates and version numbers where available
- primary-source-first
- technically dense but readable
- skeptical about hype
- explicit about what is still unproven
