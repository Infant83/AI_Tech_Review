4. Several statements in the videos should be treated as commentary or weakly sourced signals unless stronger evidence is added:
   - `SpaceX partnered with Cursor` and acquisition-option terms
   - `Anthropic quota manipulation as stealth price hike`
   - `AI-attributed layoffs x9 in 2026`

2. Part 2 is mostly about `infrastructure and operating layer`: harnesses, open-source agent stacks, research agents, specialized models, and compute economics.
3. Across both parts, the durable signal is that the market is moving from `chatbot quality` to `agent execution quality`.
