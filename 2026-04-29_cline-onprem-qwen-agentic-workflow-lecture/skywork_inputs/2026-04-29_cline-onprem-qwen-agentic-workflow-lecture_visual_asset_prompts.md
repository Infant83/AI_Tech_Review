목적: Skywork 또는 나노바나나2에서 강연 deck 중간중간 사용할 작은 spot illustration 생성  
공통 규칙: 이미지 안에 실제 텍스트를 넣지 말고, 슬라이드 본문에서 캡션으로 설명한다.

## Global Visual Language

- Style: polished flat-vector editorial illustration, subtle paper texture, technical seminar friendly
- Mood: calm, prepared, practical, thoughtful
- Palette: off-white, muted teal, dark green, graphite, small amber/coral accents
- Composition: square or wide spot illustration with generous margins
- Use: section dividers, concept explanation slides, demo transition slides
- Avoid: readable text, logos, brand marks, robot mascots, cyberpunk glow, stock-photo vibe, childish cartoon, random gradient blobs
