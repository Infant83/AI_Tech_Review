Asset: original wide 16:9 editorial illustration for a serious but welcoming science essay about chiral amplification and asymmetric autocatalysis.

A quietly astonishing ceramic scene: a large, beautifully modelled terracotta RIGHT HAND gently cups, supports and shapes a new ivory RIGHT HAND from a small mound of soft clay. Both sculptures are unambiguously right hands, with five anatomically coherent digits and matching thumb positions. The emerging hand is almost complete, still joined at its wrist to the unformed clay, and its fingertips begin to support a new small fold of clay. No outside sculptor is present: what was made is becoming the maker. Focus on the tender physical junction between a finished fingertip and the new clay thumb. This is a sculptural metaphor, not living skin or a biological transformation.

Compose the hands as one graceful asymmetrical, interlocking gesture viewed obliquely, not a pair of hands facing each other on a desk. Keep a subtle shallow LEFT-HAND impression in the clay at the lower periphery, representing the other mirror possibility. The two right hands dominate the action; the opposite impression is quiet. No rows or grid and no heap of miniature hands. A continuous handmade paper-like clay surface joins the scene and opens into generous negative space.

Art direction: sophisticated hand-painted gouache, lithographic grain and tactile clay sculpture. Broad confident shapes, strong silhouette, carefully chosen few edges, warm dusty terracotta and pale chalk ivory against a muted deep blue-green ground. A hint of soft rose in reflected light. Intimate, humane, contemplative and curious. Avoid photographic disembodied limbs, uncanny skin, body horror, clinical blue gloves, glossy CGI, stock-art sentimentality or ornate decoration. A poetic artwork whose meaning is discovered through the physical action, not a scientific diagram. Independent composition and no imitation of any existing artwork.
No text, equations, labels, arrows, atom models, apparatus, medals, logos or watermark. Opaque background.
