Use case: stylized-concept
Asset: original 16:9 editorial science illustration, about a small bias between mirror-image possibilities being remembered and amplified.

Imagine a gently lit, continuous landscape made from ONE broad sheet of pale handmade paper. Near the lower-left foreground, two small opposite twists of the paper offer almost mirrored beginnings. At a single subtle offset, one crease is carried forward. It threads through the sheet and opens a few broad connected, curved folds in the same directional sense across the scene; the other beginning stays quietly visible. The main subject is the intimate junction where a previous fold becomes the condition for the next fold, not rows of isolated objects. The increasing extent of the patterned surface conveys amplification. Give the landscape a bold asymmetrical silhouette and a dramatic quiet opening through which the dark background is seen.

Medium: sophisticated hand-painted gouache with finely drawn paper sculpture, tactile matte surfaces, economical edges and atmospheric depth. Warm ivory folded paper against deep inky mineral-blue space, with one restrained copper-ochre ink stain along the carried crease. Broad expressive strokes in the background, a few precise luminous edges at the focal fold. Contemplative and inviting, with a surprising physical gesture and generous breathing space. Avoid the look of a photorealistic origami catalogue, stock abstract wallpaper, CGI metal, or a technical model. Make the picture feel artistically resolved, with strong shape design and rhythm rather than a pile of details.

The image is a visual metaphor for feedback and the persistence of a tiny bias in a chemical reaction network. It is not an exact reaction apparatus, real crystal growth or data plot. Do not add a hand, external folding machine, mirror, snail, repeated helix specimens, arbitrary molecular bonds, glowing network or huge isolated molecule. No text, labels, numbers, symbols, equations, logo or watermark. Opaque background.
