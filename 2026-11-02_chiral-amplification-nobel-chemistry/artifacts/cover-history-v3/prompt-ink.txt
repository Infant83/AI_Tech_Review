Use case: stylized-concept
Asset: original 16:9 editorial illustration for an accessible science essay about chiral amplification: a slight initial imbalance between mirror-image possibilities changes the composition made later by a reaction network.

Create ONE poetic, compelling scene: a single warm ivory sheet of paper bends through a gentle fold across a midnight-blue, painterly space. On the near, quiet corner of the sheet there are just a few small opposing curled ink marks, almost balanced. An imperfect amber trace continues from one of those marks across the fold; beyond it, new marks of the same curled sense emerge into an elegant, loose, asymmetric sweep. A few opposing blue marks remain. A newly formed mark itself starts the next short trace, so the paper seems to be continuing its own drawing. This visible contact between an earlier result and a new beginning is the focal event. Individual ink marks keep a similar size; a wider area and increasing predominance carry the idea of amplification. No arrow or diagram. Do not draw the subject as a specimen array.

Art direction: a crafted, confident gouache and lithographic illustration with broad, beautifully judged colour shapes, expressive economical line, fine paper grain and a little translucent ink. The paper has physical depth but remains drawn rather than a glossy product rendering. Let the strongest contrast sit at the fold where the earlier mark meets the new drawing. One large sweeping silhouette, a quiet region, and a few sharp details; keep substantial negative space. Deep indigo and soft mineral blue, warm ivory, restrained burnt-orange/amber marks. Emotion: curiosity, quiet wonder and the feeling that a small event leaves a lasting memory. Rich dark/light balance without black menace. Wide landscape, clear at thumbnail scale. Make it visually arresting as an independent artwork.

Scientific role: an imaginative metaphor for a reaction remembering and amplifying a small bias, not a measured population or literal molecular picture. No giant growing individual molecule, rigid grid, repeated metal ribbons, snail, cracked mirror, physical mirror, laboratory machine, stamp or person directing the process. No typography, letters, formulae, numbers, arrows, labels, medals, logo or watermark. Opaque background.
