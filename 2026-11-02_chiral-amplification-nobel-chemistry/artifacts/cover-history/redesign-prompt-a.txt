Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a sophisticated, original 16:9 editorial science cover about chiral amplification. Create a visually surprising artwork, with the restraint, conceptual clarity and material richness of a carefully art-directed magazine cover.
Primary concept: a large, asymmetric plume of order is composed entirely of tiny units that remain the SAME physical size. Each unit is an abstract three-dimensional folded three-petal rosette, each petal pitched and curled consistently in one handed direction; a second kind is its mirror-handed counterpart. At the narrow beginning, the two handed kinds are almost balanced. Across the broad unfolding plume, one handed kind becomes overwhelmingly predominant, with a few opposite units still visible. The large form is made by a greater NUMBER and changing proportion of equal-size units, never by making individual units larger. Small clusters at the open, advancing edge make room for newly arriving units of the predominant hand, suggesting products joining the process that makes more products.
Composition: a single asymmetric fan or plume sweeps diagonally through the right two thirds, with a deliberate, sharply shaped quiet void inside its contour. A small nearly balanced group sits near its origin on the left. Orthographic oblique view so unit sizes stay consistent. The eye should first see a beautiful coherent large form and then discover the two opposite tiny fold geometries that explain the imbalance. Do not draw a literal circulation loop or an explanatory diagram. Avoid a regular tiled grid.
Medium and art direction: intricate hand-cut rag-paper relief with subtle plaster-like depth, drypoint ink grain and crisp soft-edged cast shadows, tactile and poetic rather than glossy digital sculpture. Warm bone-white paper ground, deep restrained petrol-blue on the predominant rosettes, charcoal-grey opposing rosettes, tiny oxidized vermilion accents only where the open edge is active. Strong silhouette, intelligent negative space, a quiet light source, no decorative clutter. Preserve the distinction of handed folds through geometry and shadows, as well as colour.
Scientific identity: the rosettes are sculptural symbols for two enantiomeric populations. The artwork shows amplification of composition in a self-reinforcing population, not physical enlargement of molecules. No measured ratios, atomic model, chemical bonds, crystalline lattice, laboratory vessel or real apparatus. No giant rosette standing for an actual molecule.
Constraints: landscape 16:9, all essential relationships inside a central safe area, readable at 320x180. No text, letters, numerals, labels, equations, logos, medals, watermark, robots, brains, circuitry, DNA ladder or stereotyped floating molecule balls. No imitation of a named artist or publication. Opaque background.
