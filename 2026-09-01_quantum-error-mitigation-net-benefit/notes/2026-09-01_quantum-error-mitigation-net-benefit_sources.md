## Claim ownership and reviewer inferences

- The paper authors report all numerical results above unless explicitly identified as a review interpretation.
- Review interpretation: the two mitigation papers place different resources on the bill—physical qubits and CNOTs in the repetition scheme, shots and extrapolation variance in ZNE. They are not a head-to-head benchmark.
- Review interpretation: a fair mitigation report should preserve raw and mitigated error, run-to-run variance, total shots, number of added circuits, compiled 2Q depth, postselection rate and end-to-end wall-clock time.
- The absence of new D-Wave or Classiq newsletters, verifiable public LinkedIn technical signals, or newly finalized QML papers in major AI-conference proceedings is a search result as of the cutoff, not proof that no such item exists anywhere.
