A spin-1 site needs three states. Kumaran and colleagues use a transmon’s second excited level alongside its usual two qubit levels, making one device a qutrit. In the paper’s comparison encoding, two physical qubits instead represent |0〉, |1〉 and |2〉 by |00〉, |01〉 and |10〉, leaving |11〉 unused. Fitting the three states into one device can shorten preparation circuits, while also changing the demands on control, noise management and readout. The experiment examines those demands by preparing open-boundary AKLT states on two, three and four sites.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-1">1</a>]</span>

The AKLT valence-bond picture describes each spin-1 as the symmetric subspace of two virtual spin-½ degrees of freedom, with singlets joining virtual spins on neighboring sites. It gives a physical picture of the ground-state construction introduced by Affleck, Kennedy, Lieb and Tasaki in 1987.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-2">2</a>]</span> These virtual spins describe the state’s entanglement structure; they have a different role from the physical two-qubit basis mapping above. In 2025, Edmunds and colleagues used trapped-ion qutrits to observe long-range string order and effective edge spins in this family of states. The transmon study connects state preparation and phase estimation on another hardware platform.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-3">3</a>]</span>

On ibm_hanoi, the researchers calibrated the 0↔1 and 1↔2 transitions and placed operations involving the higher level toward the end of the circuits. Two representative four-site open-boundary circuits yielded reported Hellinger fidelities of 88.68% and 80.80%. These values compare measured and theoretical probability distributions; relative phases require additional information. For phase estimation, the experiment used two sites with periodic boundaries. As a twist was varied on one bond, tomography and virtual RZ corrections set the relative phases of the dominant |02〉, |11〉 and |20〉 components. The Berry phase was then estimated within that selected subspace. The reported values near π, obtained with three to seven partitions, belong to this corrected, selected-state procedure.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-1">1</a>]</span>

Circuit compression also has to reach the measurement stage. With the readout method used here, distinguishing |1〉 from |2〉 required extra circuit executions, and reconstructing the full ternary probability distribution takes a number of circuits that grows exponentially with the qutrit count. The extension to as many as 40 sites was a separate noisy tensor-network calculation; the projected preparation results also include coherence times five times longer than those measured on the device. Reading these calculations alongside the short-chain hardware results makes the next design task concrete: preserve the benefit of matching spin-1 to three levels while reducing the circuits and shots needed to read the state.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-1">1</a>]</span>
