A transport signature in a small superconducting chain and coherent motion of a stored quantum state require different experiments. Zatelli and colleagues address the latter by coupling two short Kitaev chains, each made from two quantum dots.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-1">1</a>]</span> The relevant labels are the even or odd electron-number parity of each chain. Four poor-man’s Majorana modes form one qubit, and pulses on a central coupling dot drive changes in the joint state.

With even total parity, the populations oscillate between |ee〉 and |oo〉; with odd total parity, between |eo〉 and |oe〉. Here e and o denote the parity of an individual chain. A representative even-sector measurement in the public author manuscript v2 reports 106.7 ± 0.4 MHz oscillations and a 26 ± 2 ns Rabi decay time at a 9.8 mV coupling pulse.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-1">1</a>]</span> These parameters come from a damped-cosine fit to conditional populations. They describe a different quantity from the millisecond-scale parity-state dwell times observed during readout.

Measuring both total-parity sectors is a consequential choice. Oscillations in one sector alone could arise when residual energy splittings in the two chains happen to cancel. At their common sweet spot, the reported frequencies are 113.4 ± 0.5 MHz in the even sector and 117.7 ± 0.4 MHz in the odd sector. Moving the chain gate voltages together or in opposite directions changes which sector continues to oscillate.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-1">1</a>]</span> This tests a model in which the sum and difference of the intra-chain splittings act differently in the two sectors. The response to detuning adds information that similar frequencies alone cannot supply.

Coherent control here does not establish a topologically protected operation. The final paper explicitly identifies the present two-site modes as unprotected, and its Supplementary Information explains that similar frequencies alone cannot establish spatially separated Majorana modes.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-1">1</a>]</span> Other weakly coupled, nearly degenerate subsystems can share the effective model. QuTech’s institutional account describes a programme that establishes control in short chains before testing improved protection in longer devices.<span class="citation">[<a href="#ref-2">2</a>]</span> The oscillations and detuning measurements provide a concrete starting point for asking which states become more stable as that experiment develops.
