Recalculation and publication scope

The Python standard-library script independently checks 20 finite-matrix conditions from formulas visible in the PDFs. Recorded results identify the environment and numerical residuals. These checks do not reproduce the unavailable original source ZIP or its original validation suites. The Trotter comparison here uses the Frobenius norm, unlike the original operator-norm table.

The public editions preserve 61 original educational pages and add one disclosure page per unit, giving 64 pages. Personalized context and file-launch actions were removed. No original email, source ZIP or third-party textbook was republished. No Classiq synthesis, SDK call, QPU execution or real molecular/DFT calculation was performed in this publication work.
