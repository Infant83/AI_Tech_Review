오늘의 진전은 경계가 선명해졌다는 데 있다

A computational-materials and electronic-structure reading of solver accuracy and whole-workflow convergence. Heron measurements recover qualitative spectral features of the final small CaCuO2 impurity problem, but not the accuracy needed for a full self-consistent DMFT loop. FPGA Hamiltonian-generation gains must be assessed alongside diagonalization, approximations and the CPU baseline. Classical amplification of Aquila solution diversity does not establish independent or uniform sampling, or faster materials discovery. The preface proposes a classical-reference test of how Green-function errors change bath updates and convergence, separating reported results, interpretation and unperformed follow-up work.
