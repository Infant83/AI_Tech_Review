Magazine-style explanatory artwork for a Korean AI technology review.

Theme: recent agent AI updates are increasingly about the working structure around the model: harness engineering.

Visual concept: six evidence clusters are arranged around one central agent work-harness hub. The clusters should be visually legible without text:
- research papers and evaluation sheets
- enterprise product announcement boards
- connector/data access objects such as mail, calendar, cloud documents
- open-source terminal tools and repository boxes
- developer workflow objects such as code branches, test checks, merge rails
- high-risk domain safety objects such as medical clipboard, boundary marker, human review stamp

Composition: 16:9 editorial infographic, central hub, six balanced clusters, thin connecting lines, generous whitespace for later Korean labels. It should feel like a refined Quanta Magazine or KIAS Horizon explanatory graphic, not a corporate slide.

Style: warm off-white paper background, muted teal, amber, soft blue, ink-gray linework, subtle texture, clear hierarchy, scientific magazine illustration, elegant and calm.

Avoid: readable text, fake logos, fake screenshots, fake UI, generic neural network cloud, dark cyberpunk, clutter, dense arrows, photorealistic stock look.
