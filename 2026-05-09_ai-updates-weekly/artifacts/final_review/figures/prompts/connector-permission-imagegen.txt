Magazine-style editorial infographic for a Korean AI technology review.

Theme: AI connectors are both a convenient handle for workplace data and a permission gate for administrators.

Visual concept: left-to-right flow in three zones.
Left: workplace data objects, including mail envelopes, calendar pages, cloud document folders, shared drive boxes, and data tiles.
Middle: a clear permission gate with keycard reader, policy sliders, privacy filter, audit log strip, and a controlled passage.
Right: an AI work surface producing a concise report-like output tray.

Composition: 16:9, three clear zones, thin arrows or flow lines, large open areas for later Korean labels. The gate should be the visual focus. The image should make the reader immediately understand that the connector is not just convenience; it is also where access, privacy, audit, and control are designed.

Style: polished science-and-technology magazine infographic, warm paper background, muted teal and amber accents, ink-gray outlines, subtle shadows, clean and calm.

Avoid: readable text, real brand logos, fake Gmail or Google UI, fake dashboards, dense labels, glowing cyberpunk security imagery, generic AI brain or cloud.
