# Language toggle lettering

The Korean mark is the shaped old-Hangul cluster `ᄒᆞᆫ` (U+1112, U+119E, U+11AB), outlined from Noto Serif KR Medium. The Latin `EN` uses Source Serif 4, matching the publication title. The button keeps its normal Korean/English accessibility name; this lettering is decorative.

Compared Noto Serif KR Medium, Noto Serif KR Bold, and Nanum Myeongjo YetHangul at the actual 64px mobile toggle width, in light and dark themes. Medium keeps the araea dot and internal spaces distinct at small sizes and balances the Latin semibold. Bold is heavier than the other utility icons; Nanum has finer strokes. Chose the quieter serif treatment in place of a system-dependent Gungseo fallback.

Source font: `NotoSerifKR-Medium.otf`, SHA-256 `87ff553e9591491f79393bc0336e8bbdfcc8d004ae1d547a303b532e8b0bb7ab`. HarfBuzz shapes the cluster to one glyph (gid 22620, `cid62679`, advance 966 font units). FontTools extracts its outline, normalizes its ink bounds to 22 of 24 viewBox units, and rounds to 0.001 viewBox units. The public HTML embeds only the outline, with no font download or platform-specific Hangul shaping.
